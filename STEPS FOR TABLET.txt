
1. Disable Chrome Tab Discarding (Android)

If you are using Google Chrome on Android, background tabs are automatically put to sleep to save memory.
• Type chrome://discards into the Chrome address bar and press Enter.
• Find your target tab in the list of processes and click Auto Discardable to change its value to False.
• Note: Older flags like Disable automatic background tab reloading in chrome://flags have been permanently removed by Google.

2. Prevent Chrome from Clearing Sites (Memory Saver Exceptions)

• Go to Chrome Settings > Performance (or Memory Saver).
• Under Always keep these sites active, click Add and enter the main domain name (e.g., example.com) to prevent those specific pages from sleeping.

3. Stop Android from Putting Browser Apps to Sleep

If Android is aggressively closing the browser app in the background, change the battery optimization settings:
• General Android: Long-press the browser app icon, tap App Info (i symbol), select Battery, and change the setting from Optimized to Unrestricted.
• Samsung Galaxy: Go to Settings > Battery and device care > Battery > Background usage limits, and add your browser app to Never sleeping apps.
• Xiaomi / MIUI: Open the app switcher, long-press the browser preview window, and tap the Lock icon to keep it active.
