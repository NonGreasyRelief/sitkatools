# SitkaTools — the Hub

SitkaTools.html is the hub. It holds the Lot, Room and Building Managers and the unified calendar, opens the DB files, saves them, and relays the DB to every open tool. Suite-wide rules and the data contract are in the root `CLAUDE.md`. This file covers how the hub itself works.

## Lot Manager

- The create form sets all five rooms: cloning, veg, flower (`destRoom`), dry and trim (dry and trim may be the same room). A split lot doesn't ask for cloning and veg rooms: it takes its parent's. It also picks a start room: the clone room by default, or the veg room for purchased clones. `currentRoom` starts from that choice.
- Only `destRoom` syncs to `rooms.<id>.assignedLots`. The other room fields are names stored on the lot.
- **List order** (user request 2026-10-29; it was newest first): the lots for flower rooms first, then the 🌿 mother lots under a "🌿 Mother lots (N)" heading, each by lot number, lowest first (`localeCompare` with numbers compared as numbers: L99 before L100). A mother lot (`lotIsMother`) is one scheduled with a 🌿 vegetative recipe; one with no schedule yet counts by its stage (Mother) or its destination (a room designated Mother); a bloom schedule says it isn't one.
- **New lots** may say who the plants came from (**Received from**, optional; `receivedFrom`), shown and editable in the lot list (📦). Every new lot keeps the count it started with (`startPlantCount`). Until a grow log records the lot, a count changed in the list changes that too. The Labour & Cost Wizard's 📋 CTLS month counts new lots as plants received and sub-lots as clones cut.
- **✕ Delete** (unlocked lots): refused while the lot has paperwork pages or tasks with hours or a sign-off ("Close it out instead"); otherwise the lot goes with everything that's only its own — its events, schedule history and transfer records (`LotActions.removeLot`; review E3).
- The parent lot is chosen from existing lots. It should also accept typed free text for a parent that isn't in the system (still to do).
- Creating a lot in, or moving one into, a flowering room or a Mother room runs the projection deletion hook (root `CLAUDE.md` → "Deletion Hook — SitkaTools").
- **🌿 Mother lots:** the stage list has **Mother**, and the destination room ("Destination Room (flower, or Mother room)") of a mother lot is its Mother room. What makes a lot a mother lot is its CropLabourWizard schedule's 🌿 vegetative recipe; nothing else is flagged.
- **🔒 Locked to its grow log** (`lotLocked`: PaperWizard has a daily-weekly page for the lot; a page with day rows counts even if its sheet was deleted, review E13): the list shows phase, current room and plant count as read-only chips ("🔒 Flower", "🔒 📍 Grow 1", "🔒 🌱 120", tooltip "Set by the grow log in PaperWizard"), and the ✕ is off ("Close it out … once its plant count is 0"). Every handler checks it too. The planned rooms (clone / veg / flower / dry / trim) stay editable: they're the schedule.
- **Close-out needs the lot's crop summary** (user decision 2026-10-26, it used to only warn): its costs, frozen, and its plant history (`_EmployeeWizard.crops[lot].ctls`), which the monthly CTLS numbers count a closed-out lot from. Close-out saves it itself when that's all it needs (user request 2026-10-27). See the 🔒 blockers under "🏢 Building Files card".
- **Split lots come only from the parent's grow log** (user decision 2026-10-27; it used to take typed counts): a split lot is the plants a parent's grow log recorded as **moved to another lot** (`days[day]._removed.moved`), **one move each**. The form lists as parents only lots with such plants no split lot has yet ("· 3 plants moved out"), then the parent's moves by day (`splitMoves`: "Mon, Oct 26, 2026 — 3 plants"). It asks no plant count: the new lot's `plantCount`, `startPlantCount` and `plantsTransferred` are the move's plants, its **Created Date** is the move day (read-only), it remembers the move (`splitFromDay`) and starts at the parent's stage (changeable). A move's plants are claimed by the split lot made from its day; split lots from before (no `splitFromDay`) and closed-out ones (their crop summaries' starting counts; review E9) claim by count, oldest move first; a day saved on two pages counts once. With none, the form says to record the move in the parent's grow log first (lower its count; reason: Moved to another lot). Sub-lots (clones) take nothing from the parent.

## Room and Building Managers

- **Room form:** name, Building dropdown (registers the room in that building's `assignedRooms` on day 1), **Flowering room** checkbox, light count, watts per light, canopy sq ft, floor area (sq ft: the building's rent is shared by it), veg / bloom light cycles (selects for 24/0 … 12/12, stored as hours on; 18/6 and 12/12 by default) and designation (Grow 1…4, Clone, Mother, Vegetation, **Dry/Trim**: DailyWizard's drying and trimming chores, user request 2026-10-29). The flowering box, lights, watts, canopy, floor area, light cycles, designation and **building** are also editable in the room's detail panel (the building since user request 2026-10-29: a dropdown that assigns, moves or takes the room out through the Building Manager's own `assignRoomToBuilding` / `removeRoomFromBuilding`, refused the same way below, and the dropdown goes back to where the room still is); the name and description are set when the room is made (there is no rename). The form's Building dropdown is refilled with every DB that opens and when the Room Manager tab is opened.
- Assigning a room to a building first takes it out of any other building. Deleting a room removes it from its building. A room with projected lots can't be deleted until CropLabourWizard clears them (🧹 Clear Projections; review E8).
- **A room holding a locked lot** (its `currentRoom`) can't be deleted, moved to another building or taken out of its building, and its building can't be deleted (`refuseRoomChange`, with an alert naming the lots): the grow log records where a lot is, and moving a lot between buildings is a room change in its grow log.
- **Buildings** have their own editor below the rooms, styled the same, with an optional **property cost per month** (rent + property fees) on the form and in the detail panel. $0 is kept (a building with no rent); blank means not set, and the cost per gram then leaves its lots' property out. Changing it asks **Applies from** (the 1st of this month by default, never after today) and keeps `propertyCostHistory: [{ from, cost }]` (price by date); the field's tooltip lists it.

## Calendar

- **Views** (a 4-button group in the nav row, wrapping on a phone; the active one is highlighted):
  - **📆 Monthly** (default).
  - **📅 7-Day:** seven day cards stacked, each with the weekday, date, "Today" badge, holiday name and task count, and full-width event rows (time prefix when `startTime` is set). Prev / Next slide one day; clicking a day's header makes it the first day; Today re-anchors. The first day gets an accent border. Title: "Mon D, YYYY – Mon D, YYYY — 7-Day View".
  - **🗓️ Annual:** 12 months with one emoji dot per task type per day (a fixed `taskType → emoji` map, else the title's leading emoji — all of it, `EmojiKit.split` — else 📌), across multi-day ranges. A legend lists up to 8 task types by frequency with counts and "+N more…". Prev / Next step whole years; clicking a day opens the 7-Day view from that day. Title: "YYYY — Annual View".
    - **🖨️ Print year** (shown only in this view, `Calendar.printAnnual`): the year's events as the filters and the building view leave them, on one landscape Letter page for a black-and-white printer. Each task type prints as a letter code (`PRINT_CODES`, fixed for the built-in types; custom types get their name's initials, made unique with a digit; no task type = `OT`), with every type in the legend with its count. Projected = `(HV)`, done = struck through, two or more of a type on a day = `HV×2` with a thick border, at most four codes a day ("+N"), holidays (when shown) underlined and listed. The header names the filters ("All tasks (no filters)" when none).
  - **🚀 Launch windows** (user request 2026-10-29, `Calendar.renderLaunch`; the rules are `launchCrops` / `launchModel`): CropLabourWizard's default recipe is a sealed 70-day cycle (flip Friday, harvest 63 days later on a Friday, the room's next lot in on the Monday and flipped that Friday), so the facility has **10 weekly windows**, one flip and one harvest a week. `LAUNCH = { SLOTS: 10, CYCLE: 70, BLOOM: 63, LEAD: 34 }` copies that recipe's figures (it lives in CropLabourWizard's code); `tests/launch-windows` checks they still match.
    - **Ten rows, one per weekly window.** Weeks run Monday to Sunday, each labelled by its Friday; a crop goes in its flip's week, any weekday. Row 1 is the week the oldest crop in bloom flipped (no more than 9 weeks back; none in bloom: this week), and the time axis runs 20 weeks from it, so each row shows its window this cycle and the next: a room's next lot, 70 days on, lands in the same row. Each crop is a bar from flip to harvest end ("🌸 1200 · Grow 2 · wk 7/9"); clicking one opens its Flip to Flower task in the event panel. A today line and the shown week's band run down every row.
    - **Crops:** every flip of a real lot (still in the DB) or a projected lot, never a 📋 copy, with that lot's first harvest on or after it (none: 63 days on, marked "est."). Mothers have no flip. A bloom other than 63 days is tagged ("8-wk bloom"). A projection whose flip day has passed (no lot made for it) is drawn hatched with ⚠️, and one overlapping a real lot in its room (waiting for Reproject) faded with ⏳: neither takes a window.
    - **Taken or open:** a window is taken when a crop in its row is in bloom on the day shown (today in this week, else the week's Monday), or one (real or projected) flips in its next launch week (the row's first week from the shown week on whose Friday hasn't passed). An open row gets "🚀 open", and a dashed 63-day bar at its next launch Friday. The summary reads "N of 10 windows taken · M open", with ⚠️ harvest overlaps (two crops whose harvests start in the same Monday–Sunday week, outlined red).
    - **🚀 Open launch windows** table, soonest first: the flip, the day to cut clones (34 days before, the default 20-day-veg recipe's Day 1), the harvest (63 days after), the **flower rooms free** (each crop in the room harvests by the Friday before, or flips 70 days after or later) and notes: a clone-cut day already passed (rooted clones only, and the next window 70 days on) and 🏛️ a cut, flip or harvest on a BC holiday (whether or not the Holidays toggle is on).
    - **⚠️ Check these:** harvest overlaps, a room booked twice by real lots, rooms whose projections need Reproject, stale projections, and lots for a flowering room with no flip scheduled (not mothers, not drying or shipped, not at 0 plants): "Generate its schedule in CropLabourWizard".
    - **Whole facility**; the tag filters narrow it (task-type filters don't apply here, and it says so), the building and room filters the rooms offered too. A building device shows only crops that flower in its building, and only its rooms. Prev / Next move the week shown by one, Today comes back to this week. Title: "🚀 Launch Windows — week of Mon D, YYYY". Nothing is stored.
  - **Clusters:** a day with two or more events of the same task type (visible ones only; multi-day events count on every day; events with no task type, such as time off, never) gets a red ring and an "N×" badge (the largest count when several types clash). Tooltips in month and annual views say "⚠️ Clustered: N× <task type> on this day".
  - Every view: multi-day events keep their range styling, completed events are struck through, and projected events are dashed and muted.
- **Tag filters** (top left of the grid, shown once a DB is loaded): "＋ Add filter" opens a menu grouped by **room, building, lot, strain, task type**. It lists only values present in events that still match the active filters.
  - Values under one key OR together (the menu stays open, ✓ marks picks, clicking again removes one); different keys AND together.
  - **✨ Useful Combinations** (`FILTER_PRESETS`) at the top are one-click task-type sets that a second click clears: All phase changes; Clone, Flip, Harvest, Clean; and 🔧 Equipment maintenance (every EquipmentWizard event, i.e. task type `equipment_maintenance`; an EquipmentWizard event with no `taskType` counts as that).
  - An event with no value for a filtered key is hidden (strict). Values are resolved per event: a CropLabourWizard real-lot event gets its room from the task's `roomPhase` (cloning → cloning room, veg → veg room, dry → dry room, trim → trim room or else the dry room, flower → flower room; an older event without one by its task type, trim and Clean Dry Room included); projected lots and equipment use their stored `roomId`.
  - Filters apply to the grid, the 🚀 launch windows (task type aside) and the terminal list ("N of M events"), are session-only, and drop values that no longer exist when the DB is replaced (`Calendar.onDbChanged`).
- **Event panel:** edits `date` and `endDate` (not on a schedule task, see 🔒 below), plus the annotation fields `startTime` and `endTime` (Notes, Assigned To, Status and Est. Hours are gone, user request 2026-10-27: nothing read them; an event that has them keeps them, unseen); the title and lot are shown, not edited (moving an event to another lot would move its hours). Only fields that changed are written; a field left blank is removed, and a blank start date is refused (review E6).
  - **📋 Duplicate** (user decision 2026-10-25, review Q2) makes a clean copy: a new id, the same task type (so hours can be logged on it), no hours, done mark, sign-off or status, `copyOf` = the original's id and "(copy)" in the title. CropLabourWizard keeps copies when it regenerates the lot.
  - **🗑 Delete** (and the terminal's ✕) is refused for an event with hours or a floor manager's sign-off (user decision 2026-10-25, review Q4: `refuseEventDelete`), saying why; a closed-out lot's leftovers may go.
  - **Dragging** moves an event and keeps its length; dragged by one of its later days, it moves by the distance dragged (review E7).
  - **🔒 Schedule tasks move only in CropLabourWizard** (user decision 2026-10-26, `isScheduleTask`): on a real or projected lot, the tasks its schedule is built from (`SCHEDULE_TASK_TYPES`: `clone_cut_prep`, `planting`, `transplant`, `flip`, `harvest`, `trim`, `retire`, `clone_cut`, `room_clean`, `tank_irrigation_clean`). Their drag is cancelled as it starts (`moveEvent` refuses too), ↻ Reschedule opens no date picker (✓ next to it still works), and the event panel shows their dates as text with the warning under them; every other field stays editable. Each says "Tasks that change the schedule of crops must be moved using crop labour wizard" (an amber ⚠️ toast, `showWarningToast`). 📋 Duplicate copies (`copyOf`) aren't part of the schedule and move freely.
- **Holidays:** amber, read-only chips with a 🏛️ Holidays toggle (root `CLAUDE.md` → "BC Civic Holidays").
- **The day rolls over** (`DayRollover`, like DailyWizard's `rollToToday`): left open overnight, SitkaTools moves to the new day by itself — checked once a minute, when the tab is shown again and on focus. What was showing today follows it: the current month (or year in the annual view), a 7-day window starting today or on this week's Sunday, the 🚀 launch windows shown as of this week, and the new-lot form's date. A month, window or date picked by hand stays put. Every view then redraws (`refreshAllViews`), so Today, overdue tasks and the task list are current.
- **Tooltip:** a crop event shows its labour estimate and the hours recorded in `labour_map`.
- **⚠️ Clone cut vs projections** (user request 2026-10-24, `cloneCutMismatches`): when a room's newest real lot (latest `createdDate`, as CropLabourWizard picks it) has its "Cut Clones to Replace Lot" (`clone_cut`) on a different day from `{roomId}-TBA-01`'s "Cutting Clones" (`clone_cut_prep`), both bars get a ⚠️ and an amber left border (month and 7-day views), and their tooltip and event panel say "Does not match projection schedule. Adjust in real lot to match distance from harvest to projections." with both days and their distance from the lot's harvest end. Read from the events only; bloom lots only (the real lot has a harvest). CropLabourWizard shows the same on the tasks (`docs/CropLabourWizard.md` → "⚠️ Clone Cut vs Projections").

## Loading and Saving

- **Opening a database** says so in a toast, "Database loaded: <file> · N events" (`toastDbLoaded`; user request 2026-10-27: the calendar page has no status bar, so the calendar gets the space). A toast the opening shows after it (a lot picked up, a folder, a restore) takes its place.
- **📂 Load DB** takes one `DB-YY-MM-DD.json`, or a split set picked together, or split files **one at a time** (user request 2026-10-29): Main.json or a building's file picked by itself (`splitFileKind`: `_building`, or Main's `_fieldStamps`, so a renamed copy works) is added to the open split files (`DbSplit.addFiles`), replacing the open copy of it. It reopens the files from `texts` plus the new one (unsaved changes: "Adding … reopens the open files, and those changes would be lost", asked first) with `open(set, { gate: false })` when the page is the office already. Building files picked before Main.json, or after a restore (no `texts`), wait in `DbSplit.pending` ("… is ready — now load Main.json") and open with it; any other open drops them. After it, a building device without its own file open gets "Now load Building-<ID>.json with 📂 Load DB…". A `Wages.json` picked by itself is added to the open split files (`isWagesFile` → `DbSplit.addWages`). The "unsaved changes… Load anyway?" question comes after the pick, and only when the pick would replace the open DB.
- **💾 Save DB** always shows the save popup with right-click **Save Link As…** links. In split mode that's one link per file this device may save, changed or not (user request 2026-10-26): the office gets Main.json, Wages.json when it's open, and the building files its split made this session (`madeHere`); a building device its own file when it was opened (if not: "Building-<ID>.json wasn't opened — load it with 📂 Load DB (by itself is fine)…"). Changed files come first; the others say "(no changes)" and write the same content with a new save stamp. The folder button saves the changed files, or all of them when none changed. The unsaved flag clears once a link is used (in split mode, once every changed file's link is). A close-out archive made since the last save (no folder) is offered again there (`pendingArchives`, `.archive-again`), in case its Save As was cancelled, until the save that records the close-out (review E5).
  - **On a tablet** a link opens the share sheet (`shareFile`, which resolves true once shared or downloaded); a cancelled share saves nothing and says so.
  - **Changed while saving:** the single-file save compares the DB with a snapshot taken when the save was prepared (`prepareSave` → `snapshot`, `commitSave`); if a tool's update came in meanwhile, it stays unsaved ("Save again to include them"). Split saves do the same in `finish`.
- **📁 Folder mode** (Chrome / Edge, `showDirectoryPicker`; browsers without it never show the folder UI). Chrome on Android has it too (checked 2026-10-29, Chrome 133): a local folder on the tablet opens and saves, but only for a page from a web address (GitHub Pages); a page opened from the Files app isn't a secure page and gets no folder mode. The folder isn't synced with SharePoint on a tablet, so it doesn't replace sharing the building file back:
  - Opening a folder loads its newest `DB-YY-MM-DD.json` (or the split files), and warns about same-day copies such as OneDrive's `DB-YY-MM-DD-PCNAME.json` conflict files.
  - Save writes today's `DB-YY-MM-DD.json` into the folder with one click. First it re-scans the folder. If a newer day's file exists, or the file it opened was re-saved elsewhere (content hash differs), it never overwrites: it asks, then writes `DB-YY-MM-DD-conflict-HHMM.json`.
  - Up to 5 recent folders are remembered (IndexedDB `folders`); the most recent is offered first on the start screen, the rest in the 📁 menu.
  - Loading a file with 📂 Load DB switches folder mode off.
- **Save stamp:** `_meta` is committed only when the save link is used. Tools work on SitkaTools' own DB and never touch it.

## Opening Tools

The tools live in this file (root `CLAUDE.md` → "Tool Overlays"; user decision 2026-10-27). The hub's side:

- **`Tools`** (`register`, `open`, `close`, `deliver`, `changed`, `push`, `run`): a tool link (`href="#<Tool>" data-tool="<Tool>"`) calls `Tools.open(name)`. One tool shows at a time, full window over the page (`#toolHost`, z-index 150: above the hub, below its save popup, toasts and dialogs), its own header the only one (user request 2026-10-27): the bar (`.tool-bar`, just **💾 Save DB** and **✕ Close**) lies over that header's right end, on its first row, and the header keeps the bar's width free (`fitBar`, rerun by a `ResizeObserver` as the header wraps or changes height: `--tool-bar-w`, `--tool-head-top` / `--tool-head-h` for the header's first row inside its padding, so the buttons line up with its controls, `--tool-sb` for the scrollbar); `html.tool-open` stops the page underneath scrolling. Clicking the open tool's link changes nothing; another tool's closes it first.
- **Fresh each open:** `register` keeps the tool's markup as in the file (`template`) and empties its section; `open` puts a fresh copy in and runs the tool's function with its own `document` (`scopedToolDocument`), `window` (`scopedToolWindow`) and timers (`toolTimers`), then `deliver`s the DB. `close` takes anything it changed without saying so, runs its `pagehide` / `beforeunload` listeners, stops its timers and page-wide listeners, empties its section, puts the save popup back (`returnSavePopup`) and redraws the hub's views if it changed the DB (`refreshAllViews`). A tool that fails to start is logged and shows empty.
- **The DB:** `deliver` hands the tool `DB.data` itself and keeps what it then holds (`toolSyncKey`: the DB without `_meta`, plus the view) as the baseline. A `notifySitkaTools()` while the tool takes the DB (`run.delivering`) is counted once it has it: unsaved if the DB differs from before (EquipmentWizard's resync as it opens). `changed` (the tool's `notifySitkaTools()`) marks it unsaved only when the DB differs from that baseline, and only if the tool has the DB (`run.received`) and it's still this page's DB (`run.data === DB.data`): an empty DB a tool swapped in of its own counts for nothing. `push` (from every `WorkCopy.flush`) re-delivers to the open tool, as `'sitkaGit_db_update'`, when the DB was replaced or changed here.
- **✕ Close may ask first** (`Tools.requestClose`, user request 2026-10-28): a tool that returns `beforeClose` with `receive` and `run` gets asked; `false` means it's asking (CropLabourWizard with schedule changes not saved) and then closes itself (`Hub.close`) or stays. Every other close (`Hub.close`, `Tools.close`, another tool opening) closes at once.
- **`subview(head)`** (a tool's `Hub.subview`, user request 2026-10-27): the bar goes over that header of the tool's own full-window view instead (`#toolHost.tool-sub`: above the view, ✕ Close hidden, the header given `.tool-sub-head` for the bar's width); `null`, or closing the tool, puts it back.
- **💾 Save DB in the bar** moves `#savePopup` into the bar (`#toolBarSaveWrap`) and clicks the hub's 💾; the click stops there (the page's click-anywhere handler closes the popup).
- **Hub code that looks page-wide** for classes tools use too (`.tab`, `.tab-content`, `.tabs`, `.main`) excludes `.tool-overlay *`.
- **`EmojiKit`** (page-wide, user request 2026-10-28): every emoji, the check of which ones this device draws (kept while the page is open, `EmojiKit.ok`), `split(title)` (a title's whole leading emoji and the rest) and `wire(doc, elements, { pick, tr })`, the picker's search, tabs, grid and click-outside. DailyWizard's task editor, GeneralEvent and CropLabourWizard's recipe editor use it, each with its own markup and styles.
- **`Tools.call(name, method, …args)`**: one of a tool's own actions, run without showing it and only with no tool open (close-out saving a crop summary). Like `open` then `close`, but the markup never goes in the page and nothing on screen changes; its `notifySitkaTools()` counts as usual.
- **Tests** (`Tools.run(name, code)`): run code inside the open tool.

## Split Files — How They Work

`DbSplit` keeps the split bookkeeping: `role`, `loaded` (files opened this session), `haveWages`, `baseParts` and `baseline` (what was opened, to diff against), `metas`, `hashes` and `stamps`. `merge` builds the one-DB view, `split` cuts it back into files with no side effects, `plan` lists what needs saving (and what can't be saved), and `commitFile` records a save. It travels in the working copy (`exportState`).

- **Overlays:** an overlay keeps its original `at` while its value is unchanged, and is dropped once the office has superseded it or the record is gone. The office stamps its own edits to floor fields in `Main._fieldStamps` (`stampOfficeChanges`, from the diff against the opened files).
- **Labour hours (`mergeLabour`):**
  - A building's overlay for `labour_map` is `{ value, at, entries: { id: time }, removed: { id: time } }`. `value`/`at` is the whole list, which older builds still use newest-wins; `entries` lists what this building added or changed, and `removed` what it removed.
  - The office keeps the same in `Main._fieldStamps.labour` (event id → { entry id: time }) and `.labourRemoved`.
  - Merge result: Main's list, plus each building's added or changed entries, minus every entry anyone removed. The newer change wins when one entry changed in two places. Main's empty days ("+ Add day") are kept.
  - Tidy: a building's update drops an entry once Main holds it unchanged (or the office changed or removed it since), and drops a removal once Main no longer has that entry, so updates shrink away after the office saves.
  - An overlay with no `entries` / `removed` (saved by an older build) counts all its entries, unless the office has edited those hours since; then it's ignored.
  - Entries saved without ids get one from their content (`ensureLabourIds`), the same on every device, in `DB.ensureStructure()` and on merge.
- **Paperwork homes:** `paperHome` (page → building file) and `sheetHome` (lot + sheet → the file holding its lot-level values, such as constants) record where each page came from. `paperTarget` picks the file for a page. All of an entry's non-page keys (`constants`, `constantChanges`, `paperBefore`…) travel together.
- **One writer per file** (user decision 2026-10-25): on the office, `plan` never saves a building file, except those this session's split made (`madeHere`, kept in the working copy; a copy from an older build counts the building files it holds but never opened or saved); a change to one is listed under "can't be saved from this device" ("open SitkaTools as that building"). The tools keep the office from making such changes: PaperWizard shows lot paperwork and building forms read-only on the office (`lotElsewhere`), DailyWizard every building's list (`canEditBuilding`).
- **Lot moves** (user decisions 2026-10-25, root `CLAUDE.md` → "Lot move"): no Send or Accept, and no record on the way.
  - `paperBuilding(db, lotId)`: the building whose file holds a lot's paperwork — its latest pickup's receiver, else the file with its newest unsigned page (`paperHome`), else its lot-level values (`sheetHome`).
  - `movesOnTheWay(db)`: every lot whose current room's building differs from its `paperBuilding` → `{ lotId, from, to, room }`. A lot with no paperwork is never on its way.
  - `pickUpMoves` (in `open`, on a building device only): each move on its way to this building gets a pickup record in this building's file — `{ lotId, from, to, room, sentAt, acceptedAt, acceptedBy (the device), paperwork (handoffManifest) }`. `promptPickups` marks it unsaved with the 🚚 toast.
  - Records keep the old names (`acceptedAt` = picked up). `split` keeps only pickups (in the receiver's file); a record still waiting from the old Send button is dropped by its building's next save (`tidyFiles`).
- **Lot hand-off internals:** a pickup stores the list of what moves on the record (`paperwork: { pages: ['sheet|key'], sheets: [sheetId] }`); from then on those go to the receiver's file (`DbSplit.handoffs`). Once the receiver's file holds the picked-up record, copies in other files are leftovers (`isLeftover`): merge ignores them. Until then the old file keeps its copies exactly as on disk (`diskPaper`). Only a building tidies its own file (`mayTidy`): `tidyFiles` marks its own file for saving when it holds leftovers, and its next save drops them. Moves accepted before this list existed leave their pages where they were.
- **Facility queue** (`facilityQueues`, `mainFacility`): a building file's `_PaperWizard.facilityQueue` holds `'sheetId|key' → { inst, at }`: each facility page the building added to, as it had it, and when.
  - **The merge rule** (`unionFacilityPage(base, add)`): `add` goes into `base` without changing or removing anything: header answers and day answers fill only blanks, a day not on file is added, entries are added by `id` (a struck-out or existing entry is never touched), a first signature is added. So any number of buildings can add to the same page between the office's saves.
  - `merge` applies every building's queued pages over Main's, oldest first (`applyFacilityQueues`); entries Main doesn't have yet follow Main's, ordered by their `at`. On the office it lists the buildings whose additions Main doesn't hold yet (`facilityPickups`); `open` then forces Main to be saved and shows "📥 Facility forms: additions from … — 💾 Save DB to bring them into Main".
  - `plan` on a building device rebuilds its queue (`buildingFacilityQueue`): the old queue, plus every facility page changed since opening (an unchanged page keeps its time), minus pages that add nothing to Main any more (`mainFacility`, from merge and `commitFile`). That release is saved with the building's next save. `blockedOfficeChanges` leaves facility changes out: they're queued additions.
  - `split` writes each building's queue as that file holds it (`facilityQueues[id]`), so the office never rewrites a building file for the queue. Empty `facilityEntries` / `buildingEntries` are left out of every file. The queue travels in the working copy (`exportState`).
  - **Limits:** two buildings filling the same blank answer before the office merges: the first one in wins. The office clearing an answer a building's queue still holds lets it fill again.
- **Closing times** (`SignIns`, user decision 2026-10-28): a floor manager's closing time on a forgotten DailyWizard sign-in is `_EmployeeWizard.closings[<si_…>] = { bid, out, setBy, at, by }` (the Labour & Cost Wizard writes it; Main.json). `SignIns.settle(db)` runs in `Tools.changed` (before the change is measured), in `DbSplit.open` (after the baseline and `baseParts`, so what it files shows as a change) and in `loadDbFromText`.
  - **Where the sign-in's file is saved** (its building's device, or a single file): the entry (`entryFor`: to the minute, a crew's person-hours, `scan`, `closedBy`; id `SignIns.entryId(sid)` = `'le_' + contentHash('signin|' + sid)`) goes where DailyWizard would put it (`place`: the event's `labour_map`, or the chore's hours; a task deleted since → 🧰 `misc` with a note) and the sign-in is removed. A closing made on that device is dropped too; an office one stays in Main. On a building device `open` then marks it unsaved with the ⏰ toast; the event's hours go out as its usual labour update.
  - **Everywhere else** the entry is added with `_pending: true` (and `_made`: which list, day or map it created, so `strip` takes those away with it). Every settle strips the pending entries first and adds them again, so they follow the closings. `withoutPending(db)` (a stripped copy, or the DB itself when there are none) is what `split`, `plan` and `snapshot` work on: they're never saved, stamped, diffed or reported as a change a device can't save.
  - **Letting go:** the office drops a closing once its building's file was opened and no longer holds the sign-in (filed, or closed by its own badge first); the next save of Main takes it out. A single file drops it once filed.
- **Records in another building's file** stay exactly as on disk, so a pickup never warns about the sender's file.
- **Folder saves** re-check each file on disk and write `<name>-conflict-HHMM.json` instead of overwriting one saved elsewhere since opening. Each saved file's `_meta` records `savedBy`.
- **Adding Wages.json later (`addWages`):** for each roster employee, the file's wage goes in wherever the current value still equals what was opened; a wage changed in this session is kept and saves to Wages.json as usual. The same goes for frozen `cropCosts` and `overheadCosts` (the Labour & Cost Wizard's money kept beside the wages, `WAGES_MONEY_KEYS`). Nothing is marked unsaved. Toast: "💰 Wages added: N employees … · M change(s) made here kept".
- **🏢 Building Files card** (Data & Files tab, with calendar import/export, lot move and close-out): Split into building files, the device link (**Set this device…**: picking another device runs `DbSplit.switchDevice`, which reopens the files as it — from the folder when one is open, else from `texts`, each file's text as this session opened or last saved it, kept in memory only — after asking about unsaved changes, and sets the page address to its link. Without every opened file in `texts` — a restored session has none, and saving after a restore adds only the files saved — it switches and says "This session was restored, so its files aren't in memory. To finish switching to …, open them again with 📂 Load DB." Files the split made that were never saved refuse the switch: "💾 Save DB first, then set the device"), the lot moves still on their way (read only: "🚚 Lot …: B6 → B66D (room) — waiting for … to pick it up …"), and close-out.
  - **Close out** is refused, with the reasons listed (`closeOutBlockers`), while the lot has plants on record, any of its daily pages (grow logs, and drying or trim logs if it has them) isn't complete (`completedAt`), or days have no grow log (from planting, or `paperBefore`, through the last page). The harvest log isn't required: not every lot is harvested.
    - **🔒 Its costs** (user decision 2026-10-26: costs are locked at close-out): refused until its crop summary is saved (Labour & Cost Wizard → 📊 Reports → ⚙️ Setup & data, or 📋 CTLS month → Lots ready to close out) as the lot is now — `sum.sourceHash` equals `lotFingerprint(DB.data, lotId)`, canonical JSON of its lot record, `CropLabourWizard` events and paperwork (the same snippet as the Labour & Cost Wizard's) — with its labour cost frozen (`cropCosts[lot].savedAt` = the summary's; with split files Wages.json must be open) and no hours without a wage, and, in bills mode, no day without a bill (`costs.power.unbilledDays`). The confirm says its costs then stay as frozen.
    - **Close-out saves the summary itself** (user request 2026-10-27): `closeOutBlockers(lot, { summary: false })` lists only what a saved summary wouldn't settle (Wages.json not open, plants, pages, days). When the rest is only the summary (not saved, out of date, labour cost not saved with it), the confirm says it's saved first; OK runs `Tools.call('EmployeeWizard', 'saveCropSummary', lot)` (the Reports' Save, `rptSaveSummaries`) and checks again: hours with no wage or days with no bill stop it there, with the summary saved. Cancel saves nothing.
  - **🔍 Its cost ledger** goes in the archive (`ledger: { savedAt, lines }`, `LotActions.ledgerOf`): the lines the Labour & Cost Wizard kept with its crop summary (`_EmployeeWizard.crops[lot].costs.ledger`, the labour lines in `.cropCosts[lot].ledger`); `closedOut` drops those copies, the summary and its frozen costs stay. A summary saved before ledgers were kept has none.
  - **With split files** (user decisions 2026-10-25) close-out needs every building's file open, and writes only Main (and Wages.json, for the ledger lines it drops): `LotActions.closedOut` adds the notice (`DbSplit.closeOutNotice`: `_closedLots[<id>] = { closedAt, archive, by, files: { building id: fingerprint }, events: [ids] }`) and removes the lot.
  - `lotFingerprint(bid, lotId, events)` hashes what a building file holds of the lot as on disk (`diskPaper`, `diskOverlays`, `diskTransfers`). `closedParts(bid)` splits the closed-out lots a file holds into `drop` (fingerprint unchanged, or a notice from before fingerprints) and `late` (changed since the archive, `closedLate`).
  - Merge ignores a closed-out lot's pages and moves in a file unless it's late there (then they show, as a lot that's gone). `split` keeps them, and the building's overlays, exactly as each file holds them, except in a building's own file (`mayTidy`), which drops what's in `drop` and keeps what's late (`buildingOverlays` keeps a late lot's updates too); `tidyFiles` marks that file for its next save and a toast says so. A late lot gets an alert on the building ("… saved after the office made the archive … kept") and a line in the office's close-out note (`filesLateOnClosed`). The Lot Manager refuses a closed-out lot's id.
  - The lot move lists (`LotActions.render`) redraw with the lot, room and building lists (so a new lot shows at once, and on every tool update). Each list is rebuilt only when what it shows changed, so the picked lot survives a redraw.

## Working Copy — Details

- **What's in it** (`WorkCopy.flush`, after every change: `markUnsaved` / `clearUnsaved` schedule it, `clearUnsaved` at once): `{ db, at, unsaved, fileName, split: DbSplit.exportState(), tool }` (`tool`: the tool open; opening or closing one writes it too).
- **Forgetting:** closing SitkaTools with everything saved deletes the copy. Saved copies are removed on the next start.
- **⚠️ Session closed without saving** (`checkWorkCopyOnStart`, `RestoreCard`): on start, a copy holding unsaved work is offered back however old, on a card over the start screen.
  - The card has a yellow triangle with a black "!" (inline SVG, not the emoji, so it looks the same everywhere) and the text "Session closed without saving on MM/DD at HH:MM. Restore session?" (local time, 24-hour).
  - The line under it shows the file name, or for split files the device and files ("Building 1 · Main + 2 building files"). If the page's `?device=` link names another device it adds "This page's link is for …; restoring keeps it as …": a restore keeps the session's own role, whose files it saves.
  - The time is the copy's `at`: usually the last change written (the write `pagehide` starts as the page closes often doesn't finish, review J4), or the close itself when it did.
  - **Restore session:** the DB, file name and split bookkeeping (`DbSplit.importState`) come back, marked unsaved, and the tool that was open opens again. A restored session saves with the save links (folder mode isn't reconnected).
  - **Discard** asks first ("Discard the session from … ? Its unsaved changes can't be brought back."), then deletes the copy.
  - Leaving without answering keeps the session for the next start.
- **😴 Reloaded:** the start check restores by itself, with no card, when the page is the same tab reloaded (`document.wasDiscarded`, navigation type `reload`).
- **Nothing overwrites a session before it's answered:** `WorkCopy.flush()` waits until the start check is done (`WorkCopy.ready`), and `pagehide` touches the copy only in a tab that wrote it (`WorkCopy.wrote`).
- **Another SitkaTools tab on the device** (a home-screen shortcut can open a second one): the start check asks `sitka-alive?` on the channel and waits 400 ms. A tab that has written the copy answers `sitka-alive`.
  - With an answer, the new tab offers nothing and deletes nothing. Its card says "SitkaTools is already open in another tab", with only OK. It covers an open tool too: OK it first.
  - When two tabs ask at once, restoring in one broadcasts `sitka-alive` and turns the other's card into that note.
- **Storage kept:** after its first write, SitkaTools asks the browser to keep the site's storage (`navigator.storage.persist()`) so a full device doesn't clear it.

## 🔒 The Office Gate

User decision 2026-10-28: so nobody at a building tablet can use the office's screens. `OfficeGate`, with the shared floor-PIN kit in its office kind (`makeFloorPinKit(document, data, null, 'office')`).

- **Who:** only people with 🏢 **Office access** (`employees[name].officeAccess`, ticked in the Labour & Cost Wizard's employee editor) and a PIN or a badge. A floor manager's PIN or badge doesn't open the office ("Only office staff can open the office — … doesn't have office access"); an office person's PIN signs nothing off as floor manager (the kit's `check` takes only the PIN of someone its kind asks). While no one has office access with a PIN or a badge, nothing is asked.
- **When:** opening the split files as the office (`DbSplit.open`, after the device is known: the page link, the device question, a folder, 🏷️ Set this device…); 🏷️ Set this device… → Office in a restored session, which has no files to reopen (`switchDevice`); and an office session coming back (the "Session closed without saving" card's Restore, and the 😴 reload that restores by itself, which falls back to the card when cancelled). Cancel opens nothing: the open session keeps its device and its link.
- **The dialog:** "🔒 Office access", the names with office access and a PIN, a 🪪 badge scan (the page listens for one only while the gate is up, `BadgeScan.transient`), and five wrong tries in a row wait a minute (until the page reloads).
- **Not gated** (user decision 2026-10-28): a building device, and a single-file DB, which opens as before.
- **What it can't stop:** it's a check in the page. Someone with the files themselves can read them in any editor, and anyone at the browser's developer tools can get past it. Who may write Main.json and Wages.json, and who may read Wages.json at all, is set in SharePoint's permissions.
