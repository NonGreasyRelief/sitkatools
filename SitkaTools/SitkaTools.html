🏢 Building Files

The database can live in separate files: Main.json (office), Wages.json (office only) and one Building-<ID>.json per building. Every device opens all of them and saves only its own. Opened them without Wages.json? Load it by itself with 📂 Load DB and the wages are added to what's open.

🌱 Lots — moves and close-out

A lot moves to another building when its grow log records a room there (or, for a lot with no grow log, when its current room is set to one in the Lot Manager). The building it leaves keeps a copy until the receiving building picks it up, which its device does by itself the next time it opens the files.
